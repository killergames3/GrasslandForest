05 / BEFORE YOU SET OUT

A little help
for the journey.

Every adventure starts with a few questions.

Ask us anything ↗
What is Grassland Forest?+

A pixel-art exploration RPG with Web3 integration. Gather resources, farm, fish, craft items and face enemies as you discover its world.

How do I start playing?+

Open the demo and follow the game's sign-in steps. Available features depend on the current version and the project's services.

Do I need to connect a wallet?+

You can explore this website without connecting a wallet. Blockchain features in the game may require a compatible wallet and network fees. Review each request before confirming it.

What can I do with my resources?+

Use gathered materials to prepare tools, food and potions. Farming, fishing and crafting help you get ready for quests and combat.

Where can I learn about the NFTs?+

Read the litepaper to learn about the project's characters, companions and digital assets. Review the terms and disclaimer before using these features.
