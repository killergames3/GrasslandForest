# 🔶About Grassland Forest and Our Vision

Present Vision: A Revolution in Web3 Gaming

**Grassland Forest** is a Web3 RPG currently in development that aims to redefine the genre by combining blockchain technology with immersive storytelling and innovative gameplay mechanics. Players do not only farm, fight, and explore, but also become active participants in a digital ecosystem that promotes stability, transparency, and sustainable growth.

Set in a vast world in constant conflict, players must raise their loyal companions, the **Lunas (pets)**, and face the fearsome **Umbrias (enemies)** to restore balance and peace.

***

### **Game Description**

Grassland Forest is a Web3 RPG focused on **farming and battles**, where players take on the role of protectors of the world. The narrative unfolds in a dynamic environment that combines strategic pet breeding, resource gathering, and exciting combat encounters.

#### **Core Game Elements**

* **Lunas:** Trainable NFT pets that fight alongside players.
* **Umbrias:** Powerful enemies seeking to destroy the balance of the world.
* **Farming:** A core system for obtaining resources and strengthening player strategies.
* **Game Treasury:** An in-game asset system designed to ensure economic stability and provide support during critical moments.

***

### **Blockchain & Network**

Grassland Forest operates on the **Somnia testnet**, leveraging its speed, security, and scalability to perform real-world blockchain integration testing.

***

### **In-Game Currency: Stability and Growth**

The game economy revolves around a **stable in-game currency**, specifically designed to support long-term sustainability.

#### **Currency Objectives**

* **Ecosystem Flow:** Integrated into in-game transactions to improve economic fluidity.
* **Stability and Security:** Protects the ecosystem from extreme volatility and malicious activity.
* **Treasury Support:** Feeds a central fund used to mitigate economic downturns, cover development costs, and ensure long-term sustainability.

#### **Current Currency Status**

* The currency is currently **off-chain**, managed by the backend to ensure control and stability.
* In the future, it will be migrated to an **on-chain token**, enabling full transparency, true ownership, and a verifiable economy.

***

### **Long-Term Vision**

Our goal is to build a universe that goes beyond the limits of a traditional game.

#### **Multiplatform Expansion**

* Initial release on **browser (PC)**.
* Future adaptation for **mobile devices**.

#### **Regular Updates**

* New battle mechanics.
* Cooperative events and global competitions.
* Social features and community systems.

#### **Community Building**

Grassland Forest aims to become a space where players form friendships, collaborate, and grow together, supported by a strong and active community.

# 🎯 Statistics Book, Statistics System, and Reputation System

The **Requirements Book, Statistics, and Reputation System** represents one of the **core pillars of the main economic strategy** within the universe of **Grassland Forest**.

Each of these components plays an essential role in the overall gameplay dynamics:

* **Statistics Book:**\
  The system allows each material or resource to have specific statistical requirements. Upon acquiring them, the effectiveness obtained depends directly on the comparison between the player’s current attributes and the conditions established by the resource. This ensures that progression is tied to the player's preparation and strategic investment.

*\*This information is available through the game’s interface tools.*

🎲**Probability Mechanics**

In addition to the core systems, an additional mechanism is implemented to calculate the probability of success when interacting with in-game resources.

This calculation is performed by comparing:

* **The player's current statistics**\
  🆚
* **The statistics required by the resource**

Based on this evaluation, an **effectiveness percentage ranging from 1% to 100%** is generated, determining the actual chance of success during resource gathering or interaction with objects.

However, even when the player reaches **100% statistical compatibility**, a **controlled variation margin of up to -5%** is applied to prevent outcomes from being absolutely guaranteed. This adjustment introduces a small degree of uncertainty and risk, fostering a **more balanced, realistic, and strategic experience**.

Together, these systems work in harmony to deliver an **immersive, strategic, and economically balanced experience**, ensuring that every decision a player makes has a **tangible impact** on their progress and on the overall ecosystem of **Grassland Forest**.

# 👾Lunas: Overview and Skill System

The Lunas are magical creatures essential to the world of *Grassland Forest*. Each one possesses unique abilities and stats that evolve as players use them in battles and explorations. The Lunas are classified by rarities, which determine their potential and power in the game: Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mystical.

## Turn-Based Battle System

Battles between Lunas and enemies unfold in a strategic, turn-based manner.

### The following points are valued in the Turn-Based Battle System:

* 15 initial Lunas cards
* Turns and Energy
* Turn Phases
* Victory Conditions

## Balancing and Strategy

* Higher rarity cards (Legendary/Mystical) have more impactful abilities, but their energy costs are high.
* Lunas of lower rarities rely on defensive or support strategies, focusing on synergy with other Lunas in the team.
* Each ability has cooldowns to prevent the repeated use of powerful moves.

With this system, battles combine strategy, a variety of abilities, and the excitement of collecting Lunas of different rarities. Players will need to balance their team, improve their Lunas' stats, and use the cards wisely to dominate the game world.

As the roadmap progresses, we will be integrating new features into this content.

# ⭐How to Acquire a Luna Pet

## How to Acquire a Luna Pet

The Lunas are essential companions in your adventure within the RPG world of our Web3 game. Each Luna has unique traits, special abilities, and a rarity that makes it invaluable within the game universe. There are 5 rarity levels, each with its own charm and potential: Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mystical.

## Methods to Acquire a Luna

1. **Participation in CBT1 (Closed Beta Test 1):**\
   During our closed beta phase, active players will have the exclusive opportunity to earn Lunas as rewards for completing challenges, reaching specific milestones, or demonstrating exceptional skills in the game. Participating in this stage will not only allow you to be a pioneer but also guarantee a special connection with the game world.
2. **Exclusive Giveaways:**\
   We will hold periodic giveaway events where players will have the chance to obtain Lunas of various rarities. Stay tuned to our social media and official communications to learn how to participate and increase your chances of winning.
3. **Random Drops:**\
   In the game, some Lunas will be obtained through random drops by completing certain objectives, exploring dungeons, or defeating the Umbrias. The most dedicated players and explorers will be able to discover these hidden gems in their adventures.
